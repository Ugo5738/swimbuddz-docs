## 1. Executive summary

SwimBuddz already swims at Rowe Park every week — coached group sessions, club training and community swims. This proposal turns that working, informal arrangement into a structured, mutually-committed partnership.

The model is simple: SwimBuddz brings Rowe Park a committed, scheduled block of recurring paying swimmers who return far more often than casual walk-ins, supplies its own coaching and safety supervision, and generates marketing exposure for Rowe Park at no cost — in exchange for lane access at a committed partner rate. Our north-star metric is the same number that grows Rowe Park's revenue: **hours in the water**.

> **The ask, in one line**
> A partner rate of ₦1,500 per swimmer per session (standard gate: ₦3,000) for SwimBuddz-booked sessions in 1–2 lanes on a fixed weekly schedule — in return for committed volume, a visibly branded community, corporate and event traffic, and weekly marketing exposure at no cost.

## 2. Why this matters to Rowe Park

A pool earns when people are in the water. SwimBuddz exists to put more people in the water, more often — which makes our incentives and Rowe Park's point in exactly the same direction.

- **Aligned north star.** We measure the whole business by hours in the water. That is precisely the metric that drives a pool's utilisation and revenue.
- **The frequency multiplier.** A structured community keeps swimming front-of-mind and members accountable. Our working thesis is that a SwimBuddz member visits 3–5× more often than a casual swimmer — the "Prime effect" applied to a pool.
- **Predictable demand, not sporadic walk-ins.** We commit to a minimum number of sessions per month on a fixed timetable, and we are happy to take off-peak windows that would otherwise sit idle.
- **Marketing at zero cost to you.** Every session is content. Rowe Park is named and shown to a growing, engaged audience — week after week.

## 4. Already proven at Rowe Park

This is not a cold pitch — it is a request to put structure around something that already works.

- **A weekly rhythm.** Coached group sessions, club training and community swims run on-site every week — Saturdays plus mid-week slots.
- **Growing, verified numbers.** 106 verified swimmer check-ins across 46 sessions between February and early June 2026 — see "By the numbers" at the end of this document.

Formalising the partnership protects both sides: Rowe Park gets committed volume on a reliable weekly schedule; SwimBuddz gets dependable lane access at a rate that lets it keep growing here.

## 5. Proposed commercial structure

### Recommended: partner rate + committed weekly volume

- **Partner rate.** ₦1,500 per swimmer per session (standard gate rate: ₦3,000) for SwimBuddz-booked sessions, settled on verified digital headcounts.
- **Committed volume.** A minimum block of scheduled sessions each month on a fixed weekly timetable, booked in advance — including off-peak windows that would otherwise sit idle.
- **In exchange.** Dependable access to 1–2 of Rowe Park's 8 lanes during agreed windows, at the partner rate.

## 10. Term & flexibility

- **Initial term:** 12 months at the agreed partner rate, then reviewed jointly.
- **No exclusivity required.** Rowe Park is a public facility — this arrangement reserves specific lanes in specific windows, nothing more. Both sides stay free.
- **Exit:** either side may end the arrangement with 30 days' notice; confirmed bookings are honoured.
- **Governing law:** the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria; Lagos State jurisdiction.

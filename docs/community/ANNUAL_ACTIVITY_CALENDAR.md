## Purpose

This document captures the operating model for SwimBuddz activities across the year:

- what happens weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annually
- which activities belong to Community, Club, Academy, or all three
- how online talks should work
- how location-based and private Club pods fit into the calendar
- how global Academy cohorts fit into the same system
- what should be visible on the website and what should remain private

The goal is to make SwimBuddz feel alive without creating random events every week. The calendar should be a repeatable operating system that drives belonging, retention, conversion, and proof of progress.

---

## Founder Context

Daniel raised three important clarifications that this calendar must respect:

1. **Community has everyone in it.** Community is not a lower tier in a cultural sense. It is the umbrella layer where prospects, community-only members, Club members, Academy students, Academy alumni, coaches, volunteers, and partners can all stay connected.
2. **Club is segmented by location and identity.** The Club is not one flat group. It can include location clubs and pods, including private, elite, invite-only, executive, women-only, alumni, or privacy-preserving pods for people who want to relate with people in their class or preserve their identity.
3. **Academy cohorts can operate globally.** The Academy is not Lagos-only. Cohorts can run in different cities, countries, pools, and time zones. The calendar needs to support Lagos first while remaining compatible with global operation.

---

## Core Thesis

The annual calendar should be a **ladder**, not a loose list of events.

| Layer | Calendar job | What activities should do |
|---|---|---|
| Community | Belonging and top-of-funnel energy | Keep people talking, bring prospects close, create low-pressure touchpoints, help paused members stay connected. |
| Club | Retention, identity, and performance rhythm | Give committed swimmers a reason to show up weekly, deepen pod relationships, celebrate progress, protect private/premium spaces. |
| Academy | Skill acquisition and transformation proof | Fill cohorts, support learners, celebrate milestones, convert graduates into Club members. |
| Cross-layer | Ecosystem gravity | Make the full SwimBuddz journey visible: join, learn, train, belong, give back, invite others. |

Every activity should answer at least one of these questions:

- Does it keep the community alive?
- Does it help a prospect take the next step?
- Does it help a Club member keep showing up?
- Does it help an Academy student become a long-term swimmer?
- Does it create content, proof, or trust for the next cohort?
- Does it create a useful website signal that SwimBuddz is active?

If the answer is no, do not add it to the annual calendar.

## Layer Definitions For Calendar Planning

### Community

Community is the umbrella.

**Includes:**

- prospects in "New Here?"
- community-only members
- Club members
- Academy students
- Academy alumni
- coaches
- volunteers
- pool ambassadors
- selected partners or guests

**Primary spaces:**

- WhatsApp Community
- public website calendar
- open swim meetups
- online Water Room talks
- social events
- Bring-a-Buddy days
- annual Wrapped event

**Primary metric:**

Community health is measured by conversation and connection, not just pool attendance.

### Club

Club is structured, recurring, and segmented.

**Includes:**

- location clubs, e.g. Yaba Club, Ikoyi Club, VI Club, Lekki Club
- public pods
- skill-based pods
- private pods
- elite pods
- executive or premium pods
- identity-sensitive pods
- alumni pods

**Important rule:**

Club activities can be visible publicly at the location/category level, but private pod details should only be visible to invited members.

Example:

- Public website can show: "Ikoyi Club: members-only Saturday training."
- Logged-in invited member can see: "Ikoyi Executive Pod, Saturday 7:00 AM, private lane, RSVP."

### Academy

Academy is cohort-based and globally deployable.

**Includes:**

- location cohorts, e.g. Lagos Mainland Adult Beginner Cohort
- premium cohorts, e.g. Ikoyi Private Adult Beginner Cohort
- global cohorts, e.g. London Adult Beginner Cohort, Dubai Premium Cohort
- online cohort briefings and Q&A sessions
- free or paid assessment days
- graduation events

**Important rule:**

Academy sessions are cohort-only. Public events can promote an Academy cohort, explain the journey, or run free assessments, but they must not blur into drop-in Academy lessons.

## Access And Privacy Levels

The website calendar should support access and visibility separately.

| Visibility | Who can see it | Who can attend | Use cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public | Anyone | Anyone who registers or is approved | Water Room talks, open days, free assessments, public socials |
| Public-listed, members-only | Anyone can see title | Active members only | Monthly open swim, community socials |
| Member-visible | Logged-in active members | Eligible members by tier/location | Club location sessions, members-only talks |
| Location-visible | Members in a location | Members in that location | Yaba Club, Ikoyi Club, London cohort socials |
| Pod-visible | Members assigned to the pod | Pod members only | Normal pod sessions, pod socials |
| Invite-only | Invited accounts only | Invited members only | Elite pods, executive pods, private dinners |
| Anonymous/private | Only invited accounts | Invited members only | Identity-sensitive groups, high-profile members |

### Private And Elite Pod Rules

For private, elite, or identity-sensitive pods:

- Do not expose attendee names publicly.
- Do not expose the exact venue publicly.
- Do not auto-publish photos.
- Require explicit media consent.
- Allow neutral event names, e.g. "Private Club Training" instead of "Founder/Executive Pod."
- Allow "location withheld until RSVP approved."
- Do not include these events in public attendance counters.
- Keep public proof at aggregate level: "Three Club pods trained this weekend" rather than naming them.

This protects trust while still allowing SwimBuddz to show activity density.

## Annual Cadence

The calendar should have predictable anchors:

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Weekly | WhatsApp rhythm, Club pod sessions, Academy cohort sessions, weekend recaps |
| Monthly | Water Room talk, Club pod challenge, Academy/prospect activation, Open Swim or social |
| Quarterly | Bring-a-Buddy Day, Buddz Cup/friendly relay, community social, Club pod review |
| Annual | SwimBuddz Wrapped, annual calendar reveal, major awards, renewal/referral campaign |

The weekly calendar should not depend on a big event every week. The baseline is: WhatsApp keeps the Community alive, pods keep the Club alive, cohorts keep the Academy alive.

## Recommended Monthly Rhythm

Use a simple four-week structure every month.

| Week | Default activity | Primary layer | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Online Water Room + monthly theme launch | Community / prospects | Usually Thursday evening. Public most months. |
| Week 2 | Club pod challenge weekend | Club | Location-specific; mostly weekends, but weekday pods can run their own version. |
| Week 3 | Academy/prospect activation | Academy | Assessment, clinic, open Q&A, cohort info session, or graduation bridge. |
| Week 4 | All-community open swim, social, or Bring-a-Buddy | Community / all tiers | Mostly weekends. This is the public-energy moment. |
| Week 5, when present | Buffer week | Ops / private / rest | Volunteer training, private event, content shoot, partner visit, or no event. |

### Why this works

- Week 1 sets the topic and creates online engagement.
- Week 2 gives Club members a progress ritual.
- Week 3 feeds Academy enrollment or learner success.
- Week 4 creates community energy and acquisition.
- Week 5 prevents burnout and gives operations breathing room.

## Online Meeting Program

### Recommended Frequency

Hold **12 online calendar slots per year**.

Do not make every one a heavy public webinar. Mix formats:

| Format | Count per year | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Public Water Room talks | 8 | Prospects + full community | Education, awareness, lead nurturing |
| Member-only conversations | 2 | Existing members | Retention, feedback, belonging |
| Academy/Club conversion session | 1 | Academy prospects, students, graduates | Move people to the next step |
| Online Wrapped / town hall | 1 | Everyone | Reflection, proof, renewal, next-year reveal |

### Default Online Meeting Format

**Length:** 45 minutes

| Segment | Duration | Owner | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Welcome + context | 5 min | Host | Set the topic and who the session is for. |
| Main teaching/story | 15 min | Speaker | Coach, expert, member, or founder. |
| Member story or case | 8 min | Member/host | Real proof beats abstract explanation. |
| Q&A | 12 min | Host + speaker | Collect questions during registration and live. |
| Next step | 5 min | Host | Join community, book assessment, RSVP, enroll, or refer. |

### Default Day And Time

Recommended default:

- **Thursday evening, Week 1**
- Lagos default: 7:00 PM WAT
- For global cohorts, use local time or run separate regional sessions.

Why Thursday:

- It gives the team Monday-Wednesday to promote.
- It feeds into weekend activity.
- It does not compete with Saturday pool logistics.

### Online Meeting Checklist

Every online meeting needs:

- topic owner
- target audience
- speaker or guest
- title and short description
- website event page
- registration form
- WhatsApp promo copy
- email reminder copy
- 30-45 minute run-of-show
- 5-10 slide deck or talking points
- resource links
- CTA
- recording plan
- post-event follow-up
- 3 short clips for social
- metrics review

### Online Meeting Metrics

Track:

- registrations
- attendance rate
- live questions asked
- replay views
- WhatsApp replies generated
- new "New Here?" joins
- assessment bookings
- cohort inquiries
- Club inquiries
- member referrals
- conversion within 14 days

## Detailed Week-By-Week Operating Template

Use this pattern to plan every month.

### Week 1: Theme Launch + Online Talk

**Default day:** Thursday evening

**What happens:**

- Monday: announce monthly theme in Community group.
- Tuesday: publish website event page or reminder.
- Wednesday: post speaker/resource teaser.
- Thursday: host online talk.
- Friday: post recap and CTA.
- Weekend: reference the talk at swim sessions.

**Outputs:**

- recording
- resource pack
- 3 social clips
- list of warm leads
- follow-up messages

### Week 2: Club Pod Challenge

**Default day:** Saturday or the pod's normal training day

**What happens:**

- Pod Leads announce the challenge midweek.
- Each location/pod runs the challenge.
- Results are compared against each swimmer's previous self, not just against others.
- Public channels share aggregate wins, not private pod details.

**Examples:**

- 50m time trial
- distance without stopping
- relay day
- fewest-strokes challenge
- treading water challenge
- endurance ladder
- attendance streak weekend

**Private pod note:**

Private pods can participate without public naming. Example public recap: "Four Club pods ran the July endurance challenge this weekend."

### Week 3: Academy Or Prospect Activation

**Default day:** Saturday, Sunday, or weekday evening depending on format

**Options:**

- free Intro-to-Water assessment
- paid assessment
- Academy cohort Q&A
- parent/spouse information session
- Club-readiness briefing for Academy graduates
- alumni story session
- graduation splash
- corporate demo day

**Outputs:**

- assessment notes
- warm prospect list
- cohort waitlist
- graduate-to-Club handoff list
- content from real learner progress

### Week 4: Community Moment

**Default day:** weekend

**Options:**

- all-community open swim
- Bring-a-Buddy day
- beach day
- rooftop hangout
- movie night
- potluck
- Lagos pool tour
- partner venue meetup

**Rules:**

- Keep the activity low-pressure.
- Guests can attend when appropriate.
- Club members should show energy, but this is not a Club session.
- Academy sessions should not become drop-in lessons.
- Track post-event conversions within 14 days.

### Week 5: Buffer

Use only when the month has five weekends or when operations need space.

**Options:**

- volunteer training
- pool ambassador briefing
- content shoot
- partner visit
- private Club dinner
- coach development
- website calendar cleanup
- no event

The buffer week exists to prevent founder/team burnout.

## Weekend And Weekday Rules

Activities can hold any day of the week, but the default should be:

| Activity | Best day |
|---|---|
| Online Water Room | Thursday evening |
| Club pod challenge | Saturday or normal pod day |
| Academy assessment | Saturday morning or Sunday afternoon |
| Community open swim | Saturday |
| Social hangout | Saturday evening or Sunday |
| Private Club dinner | Weekday evening or Sunday |
| Volunteer training | Wednesday evening or Sunday |
| Corporate demo | Weekday lunch, Friday afternoon, or Saturday morning |

Do not force all activities into Saturday. Saturday is valuable but crowded. Use Thursday online sessions to build momentum into the weekend.

### Platform Implementation Status

The first website calendar slice was implemented in July 2026:

- `GET /api/v1/calendar` is a gateway-owned read model that combines existing
  Sessions and Events records. It does not create a third calendar source of
  truth.
- `/calendar` is the public Community view for prospects and members.
- `/account/calendar` is the signed-in view with Community, Club, and Academy
  filters.
- Club pod and Academy cohort privacy is inherited from the existing
  session-access evaluator. The browser does not decide whether a private
  session is visible.
- The mobile view uses an agenda layout; desktop supports month and agenda
  views.
- Calendar records retain the activity timezone so globally distributed
  Academy cohorts can be represented without assuming Lagos time.

Still to add:

- admin operations calendar combining drafts, volunteer tasks, assessments,
  and follow-up work;
- location, online/physical, free/paid, and activity-type filters;
- RSVP and payment status directly on calendar items;
- downloadable calendar feeds (`.ics`) and Google/Apple Calendar links;
- recurring annual-calendar templates that create draft source records for
  admin approval.

## Seasonality Guidance

Use the seasonality model when planning activity intensity.

| Period | Strategy |
|---|---|
| January-February | Push acquisition, New Year goals, Academy intake, Club reset. |
| March | Convert early-year interest into habit. |
| April-May | Water safety, learn-to-swim, practical lifestyle content. |
| June-August | Retention, online talks, socials, low-pressure activities. Do not panic if attendance dips. |
| September | Back-to-routine acquisition and cohort intake. |
| October-November | Strong acquisition and Club conversion window. |
| December | Celebrate, renew, collect referrals, reveal next year's calendar. |

June-August should not be judged like January. Rainy season dips are expected, so run retention and community warmth rather than heavy acquisition.

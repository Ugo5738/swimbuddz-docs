# SwimBuddz Intro to Water Pilot

**Draft purpose:** A short, HR-friendly outline for a low-commitment corporate swim wellness pilot. Use this when an HR/People contact wants to understand the value, convenience, and measurable output before considering a full 12-week cohort.

---

## The idea

The SwimBuddz Intro to Water Pilot is a company-sponsored first experience for employees who are curious about swimming, nervous around water, or interested in a different kind of wellness activity.

Instead of asking a company to commit to a full 12-week swim cohort upfront, the pilot gives employees one low-pressure session to experience the value first. After that, only the genuinely interested employees move toward a longer program.

## Why swimming

Swimming is one of the few wellness activities that combines physical wellbeing, mental decompression, personal confidence, and practical water-safety value in one experience.

For HR, the value is strongest when it is framed around outcomes:

- Supports physical wellbeing through low-impact cardio, mobility, and recovery.
- Helps employees decompress beyond the usual office, gym, or screen routine.
- Builds confidence and water-safety skills they keep beyond work.
- Includes adults who avoid water from fear, embarrassment, or limited access.

For many adults, especially in Lagos, the issue is not just fitness. A lot of capable professionals never learned to swim properly, avoid water quietly, or carry real anxiety around pools, beaches, and boat trips. Helping employees begin that journey gives them more than a workout. It gives them a skill they keep, a fear they can reduce, and a healthier activity they can carry into their family and personal life.

The water also creates a different kind of recovery from the usual gym or office wellness activities. It is low-impact, beginner-friendly when coached properly, and gives employees a clear sense of progress even in one session: breathing better, floating for the first time, feeling safer, or simply realising they can learn.

## Why this pilot

Most wellness initiatives struggle when employees have to commit before they feel the value. The pilot solves that by making the first step easy, sponsored, and low-pressure.

HR gets a practical way to test interest before approving a full program, while employees get a safe first experience that helps them decide whether they want to continue. The result is a cleaner participation signal: people are not signing up because of an announcement; they are choosing the next step after experiencing the activity.

## Why it works for HR

- **Low friction for employees:** the company sponsors the first experience, so employees can try it with little or no personal cost.
- **Convenient to run:** SwimBuddz handles the coaches, pool coordination, session structure, and post-session summary.
- **Easy to measure:** HR gets a simple report showing attendance, feedback, and interest in continuing.
- **Better participation signal:** employees who opt into the next step have already experienced the activity, not just read an announcement.
- **Useful wellness story:** the pilot creates a practical wellbeing activity that supports health, confidence, team bonding, and employer-brand storytelling.

## Pilot format

- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Timing:** Preferably Saturday morning or another low-disruption window
- **Location:** Lagos partner pool, selected based on employee location and pool availability
- **Group size:** Usually 10-15 employees for the first pilot
- **Level:** Beginner-friendly; suitable for employees who cannot swim or are not confident in water

## What employees experience

- Gentle water-confidence exercises
- Basic breathing and floating introduction
- Coach-led safety and comfort guidance
- A supportive group environment
- A clearer sense of whether they want to continue into a structured swim program

## What SwimBuddz handles

- Pool coordination
- Professional coach coverage
- Session flow, safety structure, and teaching aids such as kickboards, pool noodles, or float supports where needed
- Pre-session guidance on what to bring
- Post-session feedback collection
- Short HR summary after the session

## What HR receives after the pilot

- Attendance count
- Participation/engagement summary
- Employee feedback themes
- Interest in continuing
- Recommendation on whether a full 12-week cohort makes sense

Individual health-relevant details, fears, or personal swimming ability notes stay private between the employee and coach unless the employee chooses to share them.

## Commercial framing

The pilot is designed to be **company-sponsored**, not a free sample absorbed by SwimBuddz. The company reduces the financial burden on employees, while SwimBuddz handles the operational work.

Final pricing depends on headcount, pool location, and venue requirements. Standard partner-pool pilots can be quoted as a simple all-inclusive package; island, premium, private, or exclusive-use pools may require a venue surcharge.

## Optional add-ons

- Standard swim caps
- Co-branded company + SwimBuddz swim caps
- Goggles
- Swimwear support for employees who need it
- Photo/video coverage for internal communications, subject to employee consent

Gear is optional and should not block participation in the first pilot. The priority is to make the first experience easy to try.

## Suggested next step

A 15-20 minute planning call to confirm:

- expected employee interest
- likely headcount
- preferred location axis
- company sponsorship level
- whether the pilot is only for feedback or intended as a pathway into a 12-week cohort
